# Code of Conduct

EveryTerm adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
version 2.1 as its code of conduct.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Reports of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour can be
submitted to the maintainers via GitHub issues marked `conduct` or through
a private channel listed on the project website.
